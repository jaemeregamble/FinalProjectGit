Discover your vibe, And The Soundtrack your Soul

This is what the Question to your Identity app is capable of:

  1. Identify your unique style and preferences
  2. By taking a short questionaire it will determine what your personalized African American Figure is and a playlist to go alongside
  3. Discover new music that matches your taste

What is the Purpose of Creating this?

The reason why I decided to create this project is to show representation of the black community by providing a questionaire to others to identity their characteristics about themselves. Whatever person they get via the questionaire they will get a playlist specific to them that will help them bond with their specific historical African American figure.

Why a Music based App?

I chose a music based app because I believe that music is a key piece to someones heart. I think that music is a way to figure out your identity and what you love and what you don't love in your life.
