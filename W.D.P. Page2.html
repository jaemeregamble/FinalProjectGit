What to Expect Using this Product

Here are some of the features you can expect to see from this product:

  • Personalized African American Figure Identification
  • Customized Music Playlists
  • Interactive Questionnaire/Survey
  • A Save System
  • Music Detection
  • A survey based on common likes/dislikes

Why these Features Matter?

We want to make sure that everybody's experience with this app is as diverse, clear, and understanding, as possible. We do not want anyone getting the same answers and results because everybody's experiences is different. Nobody's experience is the same but sometimes there can be paralells or similarities. I want this product to become something great and want this product to be used to truly find people's identiies, figure out more of their black heritage and culture, as well as provide a platform for them to express their identity through music.
